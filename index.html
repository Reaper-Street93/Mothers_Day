A note from the heart

Dear Mum,

From the moment I came into this world, you have been my guiding star — always warm, always there, always knowing exactly what I needed. You have a gift for turning ordinary moments into treasured memories, and a love so steady it has carried me through everything.

Thank you for your patience, your wisdom, and the warmth you bring to every room you walk into. Thank you for the late-night comfort, the early-morning encouragement, and every small act of kindness in between.

I am so incredibly proud and lucky to call you my Mum. On this day and every day, know that you are deeply, wholeheartedly loved.

With all my love, always 💕
